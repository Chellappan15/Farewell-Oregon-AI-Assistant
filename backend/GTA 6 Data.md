## 1.4 Delay History

GTA 6 was delayed twice before release. Rockstar first targeted autumn 2025, then announced in May 2025 that the game would move to 26 May 2026. On 6 November 2025, Rockstar and Take-Two announced a second delay of six months, to 19 November 2026, saying the extra time was needed to finish the game with the polish players expect. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick backed the decision. The date was reaffirmed in October 2026 when pre-load details were confirmed.

# 3. Story and Protagonists

## 3.1 Premise

GTA 6 follows two playable protagonists, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a criminal couple in the fictional US state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida. The story begins in the Leonida Keys and centres on their relationship and their attempt to build a better life through crime, in a style often compared to Bonnie and Clyde. Lucia is the series' first playable female protagonist of the modern 3D era. Story choices have consequences: some take effect immediately and others are delayed.

## 3.2 Playable Characters

### Jason Duval

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 3. Story and Protagonists. **Subcategory:** 3.2 Playable Characters.
- **Role:** Playable protagonist and Lucia's partner
- **Upbringing:** Grew up around grifters and crooks
- **Military service:** Joined the army to change course, but it did not work out
- **Situation at the start:** Back in the Leonida Keys, working for local drug runners
- **Housing:** Lives rent-free at one of Brian Heder's properties in exchange for helping with his shakedowns
- **Friend:** Cal Hampton
- **Turning point:** Meeting Lucia changes his life, for better or worse

### Lucia Caminos

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 3. Story and Protagonists. **Subcategory:** 3.2 Playable Characters.
- **Role:** Playable protagonist and Jason's partner
- **Hometown:** Liberty City
- **Motivation:** Wants the good life her mother always dreamed of
- **Backstory:** Was imprisoned at Leonida Penitentiary after fighting for her family
- **Trailer 2:** Opens with her leaving prison
- **Skills:** A trained fighter, shown using contextual close-quarters moves
- **Series note:** First playable female protagonist in the modern 3D-era GTA series

## 3.3 Relationship and Character Switching

### Relationship system

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 3. Story and Protagonists. **Subcategory:** 3.3 Relationship and Character Switching.
- **Shared activities:** The couple can hold hands, talk, relax at home, travel, shop, visit salons, exercise and commit robberies together
- **How the romance develops:** Through time spent together in the open world, not only through main missions
- **Gameplay bonus:** The romance gives no special gameplay bonus
- **Contact:** Jason and Lucia can call or message each other and meet up in the open world

### Character switching

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 3. Story and Protagonists. **Subcategory:** 3.3 Relationship and Character Switching.
- **Returns from:** GTA 5, which switched between Michael, Franklin and Trevor
- **Free roam:** Players can swap between Jason and Lucia at will
- **In missions:** Switching lets each protagonist handle a different part of the same situation
- **Chases:** One protagonist can drive while the other shoots, and the roles can be swapped

# 5. The State of Leonida

## 5.1 Map Overview

GTA 6 is set in Leonida, a fictional state based on Florida. Rockstar has officially named six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Rockstar describes it as the largest world it has built, but it had not published an official full map image before launch.

### Map scale

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 5. The State of Leonida. **Subcategory:** 5.1 Map Overview.
- **Vice City size:** About twice the size of GTA 5's Los Santos (reported by The New York Times)
- **Whole map:** About three times the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2 (reported by The New York Times)
- **Official map image:** Not released by Rockstar before launch
- **Leaked map:** A map that circulated on 18 August 2026 reportedly came from an older build; Take-Two issued takedown requests

# 6. Gameplay Systems

## 6.1 Wanted System

### Wanted stars

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 6. Gameplay Systems. **Subcategory:** 6.1 Wanted System.
- **Maximum level:** Six Wanted stars, up from five in GTA 5
- **Responders:** Patrol cars, armed officers and helicopters
- **Reporting:** A crime must be reported before police respond fully; witnesses, alarms and descriptions feed identification
- **Hollow stars:** Mean the police have not fully identified the player

### Escaping the police

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 6. Gameplay Systems. **Subcategory:** 6.1 Wanted System.
- **Disguise:** Masks, clothing changes and vehicle swaps help shake pursuit
- **On foot:** Alleys and backyards are the main escape routes
- **By car:** Dense traffic makes car escapes harder at high Wanted levels

## 6.2 Criminal Profile

### Criminal Profile

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 6. Gameplay Systems. **Subcategory:** 6.2 Criminal Profile.
- **What it is:** A system that tracks the player's behaviour, similar in concept to Red Dead Redemption 2's Honor system
- **Counts against you:** Needless violence and chaos
- **Counts in your favour:** Small positive acts, such as disposing of rubbish
- **Exception:** Violence during missions and robberies is treated differently

## 6.3 Combat

### Combat mechanics

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 6. Gameplay Systems. **Subcategory:** 6.3 Combat.
- **Cover:** A cover system for gunfights
- **Weapon wheel:** Returns from earlier games
- **Pickups:** Weapons can be picked up from the environment
- **Slow-motion aiming:** Highlights points on an enemy that will kill or incapacitate
- **Close quarters:** Contextual prompts; Lucia was shown fighting with a discarded stiletto heel
- **Vehicle combat:** Shooting from moving vehicles and targeting pursuing cars

## 6.4 Robberies and Money

### Robberies

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 6. Gameplay Systems. **Subcategory:** 6.4 Robberies and Money.
- **Targets shown:** A gas station, a waffle restaurant, and larger story heists
- **Speed versus profit:** Blowing a safe quickly can damage valuables; taking longer earns more money but raises police risk
- **Police:** Officers may be waiting outside
- **Stolen goods:** Sold through fences
- **Story gating:** Some story progress requires a set amount of money

### Main income sources

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 6. Gameplay Systems. **Subcategory:** 6.4 Robberies and Money.
- **Sources:** Car theft and resale, store robberies and bank jobs

## 6.5 Car Theft

### Vehicle security

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 6. Gameplay Systems. **Subcategory:** 6.5 Car Theft.
- **Security levels:** Not every parked car can be stolen right away
- **Older cars:** May need a slim jim
- **Newer luxury cars:** May need a key cloner
- **Progression:** Some vehicles are locked behind story progress
- **Resale:** Damage lowers a car's resale value
- **Carjacking:** Still possible when the driver's seat is occupied

### WAINK app

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 6. Gameplay Systems. **Subcategory:** 6.5 Car Theft.
- **Purpose:** A phone app that scans parked cars
- **Shows:** Value, registration cost, lock status, and whether an alarm or tracker is fitted

# 9. Performance and Technical

## 9.1 Frame Rate and Resolution

### Console frame rate

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 9. Performance and Technical. **Subcategory:** 9.1 Frame Rate and Resolution.
- **Target:** 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S
- **Performance mode:** None at launch, and Rockstar has not promised one later
- **Rockstar's reason:** Says even the PS5 Pro is not powerful enough for higher frame rates
- **Analyst explanation:** The dense world simulation is limited by the CPU, which the PS5 Pro barely improves over the base PS5

### Digital Foundry findings

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 9. Performance and Technical. **Subcategory:** 9.1 Frame Rate and Resolution.
- **Internal resolution:** About 1440p on both PS5 and PS5 Pro
- **PS5 Pro:** Holds 30 fps almost flawlessly; PSSR upscaling brings the image close to 4K
- **Base PS5:** Drops below 30 fps more often than expected, down to about 23 fps in demanding scenes
- **Verdict:** Called it the most visually striking game of the generation and the smoothest GTA at launch, with controls more responsive than Red Dead Redemption 2

## 9.2 Ray Tracing

### Ray-tracing features

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 9. Performance and Technical. **Subcategory:** 9.2 Ray Tracing.
- **All consoles:** Ray-traced reflections and global illumination
- **PS5 Pro extras:** Ray-traced shadows and sharper image quality through PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution)

## 9.3 Scale of Production

### Animation count

**Game:** GTA 6. **Category:** 9. Performance and Technical. **Subcategory:** 9.3 Scale of Production.
- **GTA 6:** More than 600,000 animations (reported by The New York Times)
- **GTA 5:** About 55,000
- **Red Dead Redemption 2:** About 300,000
